This is an emulator-only redesign matching the first approved reimagined display concept.

- near-black screen
- large station heading and contextual subtitle
- amber/brown Heat Index hero card
- large sun treatment for heat, blue wind treatment for Wind Chill
- outlined risk pill
- deep navy metric cards with cyan outlines
- icon-left / label-above-value hierarchy
- Today/Tomorrow forecast alternation
- clock/update footer with ONLINE/STALE
- Waveshare date and WEATHER STATION header
- all existing live data, provider history, forecast, risk, and stale-state behavior retained

Production firmware is unchanged.

# Emulator V7.11.5 — Waveshare layout rebalance

This is an emulator-only preview. Production firmware is unchanged.

## Waveshare 2.8

- The Direction bearing is moved downward to align visually with the compass icon.
- Forecast temperature text is enlarged.
- Wind, Direction, and Forecast cards are reduced from 58px to 48px high.
- The main apparent-temperature card grows to 148px high.
- The four upper metric cards grow to 36px high.
- Upper-card labels, values, and icons are reflowed to use the added space.
- Bottom-card typography is tightened to fit the smaller row cleanly.

No API, weather calculation, polling, forecast, or provider behavior was changed.
